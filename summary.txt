ServiceNow Project Summary

Project Overview

The project focuses on linking training records with employee information in ServiceNow to make employee-based reporting easier. The project involves creating tables, importing employee data from Excel, transforming and validating the data, generating reports, and displaying the results through a dashboard.
the work progress video is uploaded in the demo link and the documentations are uploaded in the GitHub.
